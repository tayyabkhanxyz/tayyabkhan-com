the longer version.

grade 7, on a phone. kinemaster, then powerdirector, then a cracked premiere pro on the family computer. i opened it, looked at the interface, and concluded — with total confidence — that i was never going to learn this. so i went slowly. i'm still going slowly, just faster.

i never wanted to be a filmmaker. films didn't reach me; there was always a wall between the thing and whoever made it. then i found casey neistat and it landed that a person could just make something and put it up. that was the part i wanted. not cinema. a person, making a thing, on purpose.

i only started editing for other people because i wanted to buy a camera. that is the entire origin story. it worked. i got the camera. somewhere in there editing became the thing that pays for everything else.

the thing i'll argue about

branding. i know it's a dull word. your typeface is you. your palette is you. the way a caption arrives on screen is you. it should be specific enough that someone identifies your channel from a still frame with the sound off — and if it isn't, we haven't finished.

everything else

i'm twenty. i've never lived outside lahore, though if all of this collapses the plan is to move somewhere in europe and become a farmer. i have been told this is not a plan.

i'm clumsy. i overthink everything. i'm a perfectionist about my own work in a way that is probably closer to possessive. i procrastinate — properly, not in the charming way people put in their bios. i like slow mornings. i drink too much coffee, which gives me anxiety, which i treat with more coffee. if you're a good friend of mine i will bully you slightly. i'm bad at driving.

if someone had to describe me in one sentence i'd want it to be someone who can't take the situation seriously. that seems about right.
