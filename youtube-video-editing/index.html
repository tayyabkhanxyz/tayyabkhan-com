the actual process

i start by working out whether you already have a look. if you do, we sharpen it. if you don't, we build one — typeface, palette, how text enters and leaves, what the b-roll is allowed to do.

most editors think the job stops at the cuts. it doesn't. get the branding right and someone recognises your channel from three frames with the sound off.

then the unglamorous half: how many videos a month, how long feedback takes, how many rounds before we're done. delivery is on frame.io so you can leave notes on the timecode instead of writing me an essay. when it's signed off i upload it for you — thumbnail and chapters already in place.

who this works for

people who are on camera and know roughly what they're trying to say. i don't take faceless channels. it isn't a moral position — i'm just bad at caring about them, and that always ends up visible in the work.

money

from $XXX a video. retainers come out cheaper per video and are how most people end up working with me.
